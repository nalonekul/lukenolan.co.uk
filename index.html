About Me

I am a postgraduate researcher with the Department of People, Work and Employment at Leeds University Business School. I joined the PhD programme in October 2024, funded by a departmental scholarship.

My interest in work and employment grew from more than ten years of experience in the hospitality and retail industries.

I am passionate about both learning and teaching sociology and hope to contribute to our understanding of the social world through my research.

Current Research

My PhD research investigates the experiences and identities of workers in UK worker co-operatives.

Through in-depth qualitative interviews, I explore how workers' lived experiences, occupations and workplace relations inform the reproduction of labour organised around co-operative principles in different ways.

Research Interests

  • Just transitions from an ecological economics perspective
  • Occupational identities
  • Democratic participation and autonomy in the workplace
  • Participatory research methods
